\section{Community Comparability}
\label{sec:appendix-content}

We characterize post content with embeddings from
\texttt{BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5} \citep{bge_embedding} and
\texttt{intfloat/e5-base-v2} \citep{wang2022text}, and audiences with
user intersections and Jaccard overlap in the aligned calendar window.

Post-text embeddings overlap in UMAP (Figure~\ref{fig:umap}), but source
classification exceeds chance (ROC-AUC: BGE $0.613$, E5 $0.568$).
MMD and energy-distance tests reject equal distributions under both encoders
(permutation $p=.005$). Topic distributions also differ (Jensen--Shannon
distance: $0.187$/$0.206$): AITAH emphasizes intimate relationships, whereas
AITA includes more everyday disputes over resources and obligations.
Shared stories therefore provide contextual comparisons, not equivalent
audiences or a natural experiment.

\section{Certainty Profiles and Trends}
\label{sec:appendix-certainty-progress}

Profiles use the fixed-stage eligibility rule in
Section~\ref{subsec:method-temporal}. A comment at zero-based rank $r$
among $m$ retained first-day verdicts is assigned to stage
$\lfloor10r/m\rfloor$. Resampling retains each post's complete sequence.
Pointwise intervals use $2{,}000$ post-bootstrap
replicates for all agreement levels (seed $20261007$) and $4{,}000$ for the
high-agreement AITA overview (seed $20261008$). The latter includes separate
majority/blaming/defending cohorts, not a common matched set. Its display
range is $3.2$--$3.9$ on the original $1$--$4$ scale.

Within each all-agreement community--direction cohort, we fit
$\overline C_{p,s}=\alpha_p+\beta(s/9)+\epsilon_{p,s}$ for $s=0,\ldots,9$,
weighting post-stages equally. Thus $\beta$ is the fitted first-to-last-stage
change, not an hourly slope. Intervals use $20{,}000$ whole-post percentile
resamples; two-sided tests use post-cluster standard errors and $G-1$
degrees of freedom. Holm correction covers four slopes; displayed intervals
are unadjusted. Separate slopes do not test between-group differences or
imply monotonic or within-person change. Fixed cohorts favor discussions
with persistent scored expression; available-post profiles have different,
potentially changing compositions.

\section{Cumulative Minority Participation}
\label{sec:appendix-cumulative-participation}

High-agreement $200$-verdict posts contribute retained top-level judgments
with valid nonnegative elapsed times below $24$ hours. At each hourly
checkpoint, cumulative minority counts are divided by cumulative verdict
counts pooled across the cohort, using final alignment. Empty hours add
no counts. These are not post-equal or window-specific shares and have no
uncertainty bands; cumulative aggregation can obscure later variation.

\section{OP Participation}
\label{sec:appendix-op-participation}

Author matching includes top-level and nested comments regardless of verdict,
excluding missing/deleted/removed OP identities and AutoModerator. Within
$0.05$-wide YA-share bins, presence is the post fraction with any OP comment
(pointwise $95\%$ Wilson intervals). First-comment timing is conditional
on presence; each participating post contributes once.

OP presence is $81.0\%$ in AITA and $77.0\%$ in AITAH; among participating
posts, $79.7\%$ and $75.1\%$ have an OP comment within one hour.
Lower presence near unanimous YA is an association, not evidence of
blame-induced withdrawal: OP participation can precede the final verdict.
